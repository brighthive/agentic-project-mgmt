# Nestlé global rollout plan

The objective is expansion across all agreed Nestlé divisions after the six-week
trial. Obtain the authoritative division/region inventory from the account team.
No division names, technology stack, residency requirements or deployment locations
are assumed here. Keep the full inventory visible, including divisions not yet ready.

## Division register

No division has been enrolled in this room yet. Add one row per agreed division and
region; distinguish legal entities, business units and brands where it affects access.

| Division / region | Sponsor and operator | Priority data product / decision | Systems and authorized workspace | Residency / access constraints | Wave / status / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awaiting account-team inventory | Unassigned | Unconfirmed | Unconfirmed | Unconfirmed | Not enrolled |

Each row must link its local acceptance criteria, support owner and decision record.
An enterprise-level sponsor does not substitute for local operational ownership.

## Proposed expansion sequence

| Stage | Purpose | Evidence required to proceed |
|---|---|---|
| Trial | Establish value and operating controls on the agreed pilot scope | Signed six-week scorecard and named sponsor decision |
| First expansion wave | Demonstrate transfer to a distinct approved division/region | Local context/access setup, repeatable journey, isolation checks and local acceptance |
| Further waves | Cover every division in the agreed inventory | Per-division gates, capacity/cost review and support readiness |
| Global operations | Sustain service across the enrolled estate | Portfolio coverage, local accountability, service reviews and verified recovery |

Wave sizes and dates depend on agreed workloads and operating capacity. The inventory
defines global completeness; passing one pilot or reading all available signals does
not prove coverage of every division.

## Gates for each division

- Business sponsor accepts the selected outcomes, baselines and thresholds.
- Technical owner confirms sources, lineage, data contracts and workspace identity.
- Security owner accepts identity, tenant isolation, classification, residency and access.
- Run evidence demonstrates terminal completion, checked outputs and relevant denials.
- Operations owner receives actionable alerts, with agreed response and escalation coverage.
- Repair approval, audit, rollback and recovery work under the division's policy.
- Usage/cost and capacity evidence supports the intended workload and concurrency.
- Local owner accepts the handoff; the accountable sponsor authorizes the rollout.

Hold a wave when a required gate fails; record the affected divisions, owner, evidence
and remediation date. Scope any rollback to its authorized deployment and data impact.

## Global operating view

Track coverage by division and data product: expected monitors versus active monitors,
last actual check, skipped/blocked work, unresolved incidents, owner, time to detect,
time to recover, approval age, delivered outcomes, adoption and attributable cost.
Every aggregate must expose its denominator and missing contributors. Avoid an
all-clear when sources or divisions were not checked.

This is an operational plan. It provisions no tenant, grants no access, sends no
client notification and authorizes no production change. The global deployment
decision remains separate from creating this trial room.
